# Product Vision

FactX v1 is a desktop portfolio application for internal receipt and invoice control in small businesses.

## Problem

Small businesses often receive invoices, receipts, quotes and payment evidence across folders, email, chat apps and paper. The immediate problem is not accounting automation. The problem is keeping documents organized, searchable and connected to supplier and payment status.

## Target Audience

FactX v1 targets small businesses in general: shops, service providers, food businesses and other operations that need a simple internal control tool.

## What FactX Is Not

FactX is not an ERP. It will not manage inventory, payroll, tax filing, sales channels or accounting rules.

FactX is not an accounting system. It will not replace an accountant, official fiscal software or legal bookkeeping.

## FactX v1 Scope

FactX v1 focuses on:

- suppliers;
- documents and receipts;
- local attachments;
- simple states;
- filters;
- CSV export;
- a minimal dashboard;
- simple payments if they remain easy to explain.

FactX v1 excludes login, roles, OCR, scanning, sync, AI, SaaS and installer polish.

## Future Vision

v2 can deepen debt and payment workflows.

v3 can become a local mini DMS with stronger file management and document history.

v4 can become an installable local product for real small-business use.
